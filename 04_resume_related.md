## Tips for Resume-Based Answers

- Use the **STAR format**: Situation → Task → Action → Result
- For every tool/technology on your resume, prepare:
  - Why you chose it
  - A specific problem it solved
  - Any tradeoffs or challenges
- Be honest about self-assessment scores — follow up with "and here's what I know well"
- For LLMs used: mention specific models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Titan, etc.) and context
- For vector DBs: mention FAISS, Pinecone, ChromaDB, Weaviate — whichever you've touched
